Privacy Architecture

How we protect your data and uphold AI ethics in the SHOORA AI ecosystem.

01. Data Integrity

Your API usage and organizational data are encrypted using military-grade AES-256 protocols. We ensure that your information is only accessible by authorized systems for billing and monitoring.

Security

Continuous monitoring and zero-trust architecture protect the portal from unauthorized access.

AI Ethics

We do not use your data to train external third-party models without explicit anonymization.

02. Developer Rights

As a developer, you have the absolute right to request data erasure or rectification at any point. Your API logs are stored for a maximum period of 30 days for operational auditing purposes.
